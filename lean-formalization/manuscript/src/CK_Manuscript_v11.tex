\begin{abstract}
We present an analytic proof candidate for the Courtade--Kumar inequality, together with a dimension-independent stability estimate. The argument is organized around three statements that can be used separately. A restriction--mixing criterion converts a two-posterior entropy inequality into a bound in every dimension. An entropy-product comparison identifies the symmetric pair as an extremizer for the product of mixing gain and post-mixing entropy. A spectral-cap certificate converts a scalar entropy envelope into an information bound. At low noise, the first two statements control all output means by induction. At complementary noise, an antipodal identity and a polynomial moment bound supply the spectral cap. Retaining the mixing surplus gives a second induction and an explicit bound on distance to the nearest coordinate. Complete scalar proofs, exact finite-constant bounds, and examples are included. The paper uses no specialized external theorem as a premise.
\end{abstract}
