\begin{abstract}
Let $X$ be a uniform random bit string and $Y$ be obtained
by copying $X$ and then flipping each of its bits with
probability $p$ independently. Let $B$ be a random bit
obtained as a function of $Y$. We show that 
$$\muti{B}{X}\le \muti{Y_1}{X}$$
for all $p\in[0,1]$, which resolves a conjecture posed by
Courtade and Kumar in 2013.
\end{abstract}
